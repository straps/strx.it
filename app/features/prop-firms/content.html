Method: every plan is scored on step count (instant 3, 1-step 2, 2-step 1, 3-step 0) plus up to 1 point for each of 8 criteria: EA allowed, copy trading between own accounts, no consistency rule, static (non-trailing) drawdown, few rules, swap-free, overnight/weekend holding, news trading. "EA allowed" means your own EAs, or EAs you hold the source code of. Prices marked "verified" were read at the firm's checkout; "calculated" means list price minus the code; third-party figures are flagged as such. FundedNext Stellar Instant 10K: rules

From help.fundednext.com (Stellar Instant account, EA policy, VPN/VPS policy). Price checked at checkout in mid-November.

Price

  • List $299.99; with NOVINS (-30%) $209.99
  • EA add-on +$30 (about $239.99 in total); VPS & EA +$60; swap-free +10%
  • The fee is never refunded; a reset costs 10% less than the original price

Risk limits

  • 6% trailing max loss ($600): starts at $9,400, rises with profit, capped at the starting balance
  • No daily limit, no profit target, no minimum days, no consistency rule
  • Open-risk cap of 3% across all positions; leverage and lot caps not published in the article

Payouts

  • On demand at +5% growth (eligibility checked at end of day; placing a trade disables the transfer until the next check). Between +1% and +5%: every 14 days. Below 1%: not eligible
  • 70% split in tiers 1-2, 80% from tier 3. Withdrawing the whole share in one tier-1 cycle means 7% growth, below the 10% needed to scale up, and can breach the account
  • Scale-up to 10x after cumulative withdrawals of 10% per tier; allocation cap $20,000 in Instant

EA, VPS, strategies

  • EAs allowed under $50K with the paid add-on; they must be customised, no EA built to pass challenges, none identical across accounts, none tied to Telegram or WhatsApp
  • VPN and VPS allowed (VPS needs the paid add-on); one device and IP is recommended
  • Copy trading only between your own Instant accounts
  • News: trades within 5 minutes of a high-impact release on a correlated pair count 40% of their profit; losses count fully. Quick Strike applies below 30 seconds

IF Micro PRO (Instant Funding): rules

From help.instantfunding.com (IF Micro Pro, prohibited practices, martingale, hedging, news, max lots). Price from third-party sites: $95 to $109 for 10K, with a possible -37.5% code; not checked at the checkout. The program was renamed from "IF Micro" in September 2026.

Risk limits

  • 6% static drawdown ($600), floor fixed at $9,400: the only instant plan I found with a non-trailing drawdown
  • Daily loss 4% (some third parties say 3%), recalculated at 17:00 EST on the higher of balance and equity
  • Max 1% risk per trade idea (same symbol, same direction; reopening within 10 minutes counts as the same)
  • Max lots on 10K: FX 4, commodities and metals 0.30, indices 2, crypto 1. Breaking it means a full account reset

Payouts and growth

  • No profit target, minimum days or time limit; 80% split (90% with add-on); payout on demand
  • Needs at least 5% net profit ($500) and a best day no larger than 15% of total profit (20% with an add-on)
  • Scaling: +25% balance after +10% in 90 days, up to +100%. 60 days of inactivity close the account

EA and strategies

  • Own EAs allowed; public third-party EAs restricted
  • Martingale fully permitted on IF Micro Pro; hedging inside one account is fine, across accounts is forbidden
  • Forbidden: grid, one-sided bets, profitable trades held 60 seconds or less (HFT), copy trading from others
  • News trading allowed by default (straddling forbidden). Weekend holding: the Micro Pro page says included, a general article says positions are flattened without the add-on: confirm with support

Verdict

The 1-Step 10K at $84.70 has a static drawdown and a low price, but the weekend ban and the thin track record make it a cheap test rather than a first choice. Cross-cutting traps

Signals and third-party copy

Forbidden almost everywhere. FundedNext forbids EAs tied to Telegram, The5ers forbids copying other people's signals. Tradeify247 allows services you hold exclusive rights to.

Pyramiding, grid, martingale

The5ers: bulk trading and one-sided bets forbidden. IC Funded: martingale and grid forbidden. IF Micro Pro: martingale permitted, grid forbidden. FundingPips: 2-3% risk per trade idea.

VPS

FundingPips forbids VPN and VPS. IC Funded allows them but checks IP changes. The5ers, FundedNext (paid add-on), FTMO and Tradeify allow them.

Payouts

The5ers: 2-3% fee and first payout after 14 days. FundedNext Instant: 70% split in tiers 1-2. UZO: reward caps. IC Funded: user reports of accounts closed at payout requests (unverified).

Axi Select: not a prop firm, a broker program

Axi Select needs no fee: you deposit at least $500 on a real Axi account and Axi allocates its own capital as you progress through six stages (0% split on Seed, then 40% to 80%, up to $1M). It is not comparable to the plans above: no challenge fee, but your own money is at risk, payouts are monthly and only if the allocation account is flat and in profit at month end, a mid-month withdrawal forfeits the month's profit, and scalping is prohibited. Own EAs are allowed. The maximum-loss figure differs between the official PDF (10%) and the website (7%).
